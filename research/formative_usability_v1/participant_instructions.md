# Participant instructions

Thank you for helping evaluate a developer artifact. We are testing the
artifact, not you. Some questions are intentionally easy to misread; uncertainty
and criticism are useful findings.

You will complete two blocks. In one block you will inspect raw MCP Tool JSON;
in the other you will inspect a generated annotation-audit report. The Tools
will differ between blocks. You may use the supplied reference card throughout.
Do not use web search, source code, another person, or an AI assistant during a
timed block.

For each Tool:

1. classify each annotation's origin as `explicit` or `MCP default`;
2. give its effective boolean value;
3. mark whether it is applicable;
4. decide whether it is a source-review candidate under the task definition;
5. answer the omission/vulnerability question; and
6. rate your confidence from 1 (guessing) to 5 (certain).

“Source-review candidate” means a field whose implementation may be worth
inspecting to decide whether an explicit value would improve precision. You
are not being asked to infer the Tool's actual behavior from its name or
description.

Please think aloud: say what you are looking for, what you expect a label to
mean, and where you hesitate. The moderator can clarify the mechanics of the
worksheet but cannot explain annotation semantics or tell you whether an answer
is correct. You may revise an answer until you say the case is complete.

There is a 20-minute limit per four-Tool block. Accuracy matters more than
rushing. Tell the moderator when each case is complete.
